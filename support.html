Common Questions

On the PIN entry screen, tap Forgot PIN? and enter the email or phone number linked to your account. We'll find your username and let you set a new PIN right away.
On the username entry screen, tap Forgot your username? and enter the email or phone number you registered with. We'll look it up and fill it in for you.
The daily limit of 5 keeps things fair and competitive. It resets automatically each day at midnight so you're always ready for the next battle.
Streaks reset if you miss a day without logging. Unfortunately we can't manually restore streaks — but your all-time best streak is always saved on your profile!
Go to your iPhone's Settings → FlushWars → Notifications and make sure notifications are enabled. Also check that Background App Refresh is turned on so activity updates can reach you.
Go to Settings inside the app and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data. If you have trouble, email us and we'll handle it within 48 hours.
Add a friend, then tap ⚔️ Battle on their name to send a challenge. Once they accept, you'll both appear on each other's leaderboard. Weekly counts reset every Monday — the highest count wins the week!
